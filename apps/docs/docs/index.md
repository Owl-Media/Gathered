## Welcome to Gathered Support

Gathered is an RSVP platform for small private events — baby showers, christenings, birthdays, anything with a guest list you can name. You create the event, add guests one at a time, and every guest gets their own private link. They confirm their email address, tell you whether they are coming, choose from the menu and leave a message. You get the replies, the dietary requirements, planning exports for the caterer, and a keepsake PDF of everything your guests wrote.

There is no public guest list, no shared RSVP link, and no guest accounts. Nobody who opens an invitation learns who else was invited.
