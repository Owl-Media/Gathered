# Getting Started

Gathered is built around one idea: a guest list is private. Rather than one RSVP link that anyone can open, each guest gets a link of their own — and holding that link is still not enough to reply. They also have to confirm the email address the invitation was issued to.

Everything else follows from that. There is no public directory of events, no attendee list, and no accounts for guests to create.

This section gets you up and running:

- [Create Your Account](/getting-started/create-your-account) — registering as an organiser.
- [Create Your First Event](/getting-started/create-your-first-event) — the event form, field by field.
- [Your Dashboard](/getting-started/dashboard) — where your events live and what the counts mean.

## How Gathered is organised

There are three places you will spend time:

- **Your dashboard** — every event you have created, each with its reply counts and quick actions.
- **An event** — six sections covering details, images, menu, guests, responses and exports.
- **The guest's view** — the public event page and each guest's private invitation page. You can [preview](/invitations/preview) the guest journey at any time without sending anything.

## What lives on an event

Each event you create has its own management page with six sections:

| Section | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Details](/events/details) | Name, date, times, timezone, venue, description, RSVP deadline, contributions and artwork |
| [Images](/events/images) | The header banner and the round profile image |
| [Menu](/events/menu) | Courses and the options guests choose from |
| [Guests](/events/guests) | Your guest list, their private links, and invitation emails |
| [Responses](/events/responses) | Who has replied, what they chose, and manual edits |
| [Exports](/events/exports) | The planning PDF, the CSV, and the keepsake messages PDF |

## The shape of a typical event

1. [Create the event](/getting-started/create-your-first-event) with the date, venue and RSVP deadline.
2. Add your [menu](/events/menu), if you are serving food and want guests to choose.
3. Upload a [header and profile image](/events/images), or keep the built-in artwork.
4. [Preview](/invitations/preview) what a guest will see.
5. Add your [guests](/events/guests) one at a time.
6. Send each guest their [invitation email](/invitations/emails), or copy their private link and share it yourself.
7. Watch the replies land on the [Responses](/events/responses) tab.
8. When replies close, download the [planning exports](/events/exports) for the caterer — and the keepsake PDF for yourself.

## What Gathered deliberately does not do

Some absences are decisions, not gaps:

- **No plus-ones or group invitations.** One invitation is one named person.
- **No guest accounts.** Guests never register or set a password.
- **No public attendee list**, and no page that shows how many people are coming.
- **No reminder emails**, and no automatic confirmation email after a guest replies. Nothing is sent to a guest unless you press the button.
- **No ticketing or payments.** [Contributions](/contributions/index) are a ledger for money handled elsewhere; no money moves through Gathered.
