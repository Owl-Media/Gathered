# Create Your Account

Only organisers have accounts in Gathered. Guests never register, never choose a password and never sign in — they use the private link you send them.

## Registering

From the home page, choose **Create your first event**, or go straight to `/register`. You will be asked for three things:

- **Your name** — this appears in the invitation emails you send ("Alex Morgan has invited you to…"), so use the name your guests will recognise.
- **Email address** — used to sign in, and where password reset emails go.
- **Password** — at least 10 characters. A memorable phrase works well, and is far stronger than a short password with a symbol bolted on.

Press **Create account** and you are signed in immediately and taken to your [dashboard](/getting-started/dashboard). There is no confirmation email to wait for and no address to verify.

If the email address already has an account you will see *"An account with that email address already exists."* Sign in instead, or [reset the password](/account/index#forgotten-password) if you have forgotten it.

::: info One organiser per event
An event belongs to exactly one organiser. There is no way to add a co-organiser or share management access, and no organiser can see another organiser's events. If two people need to manage the same event, they need to share one account.
:::

## Signing in

Go to `/login` and enter your email address and password.

If anything is wrong you will see a single message: *"Those details do not match an active account."* That is deliberate — the same wording covers a wrong password, an address that has never registered, and an account that has been disabled, so the sign-in page cannot be used to work out who has an account here.

Repeated failed attempts are rate limited. After ten in fifteen minutes you will be asked to wait a few minutes before trying again.

## Staying signed in

A session lasts 30 days, and is extended automatically whenever you use the app after the halfway point. In practice, if you visit at least once a fortnight you will stay signed in.

**Sign out** is in the top right of every organiser page. Signing out ends that session only; you are not signed out on your other devices.

Changing your password *does* sign you out everywhere. See [Your Account](/account/index).

## What comes next

Your dashboard starts empty, with a single prompt to create your first event. Head to [Create Your First Event](/getting-started/create-your-first-event).
