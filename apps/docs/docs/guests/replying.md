# Replying to an Invitation

Once a guest has [confirmed their email address](/guests/index#confirming-it-is-them), the reply form appears in place of the confirmation prompt.

![The guest RSVP form showing the event details, a yes/no choice, radio buttons for each menu course, an allergies field marked "only the organiser sees this", and an optional message to the parent-to-be.](/images/guests/rsvp-form.png)

## Can you make it?

Two large cards, one choice:

- **Yes, I'll be there** — *Wouldn't miss it*
- **Sorry, I can't** — *Send my love*

Everything after this depends on which one is chosen.

## What would you like to eat?

Shown only to a guest who is coming, and only if the organiser has built a [menu](/events/menu).

> Please choose one from each course.

Each course lists its options, with any description and dietary label beneath the name. **Exactly one option per course** is required — a reply cannot be sent with a course left blank, and the error names the course:

> Please choose an option for Main.

A guest who declines is not shown the menu at all.

## Allergies and dietary requirements

Shown only to a guest who is coming. Optional, up to 1,000 characters, with a character counter.

> *Anything we should know when planning the food.* Only the organiser sees this.

This is the guest's own words, separate from the dietary labels on the menu options. Somebody can choose the vegetarian tart and still need to say they cannot have sesame.

The organiser sees it highlighted in yellow on their [Responses](/events/responses) tab, and it appears in both planning exports so it reaches the caterer. It is never shown on the public page, never shown to other guests, and never included in the keepsake PDF.

## Leave a message for the parent-to-be

Optional, up to 1,000 characters, with a character counter. Available whether the guest is coming or not — somebody who cannot make it can still send their love.

> *A wish, a memory, or a bit of advice…* Your message is private to the organiser, who may keep it as a keepsake.

Messages go to the organiser only. They appear on the Responses tab, in the planning exports, and in the [keepsake PDF](/events/exports#keepsake-messages-from-your-guests) — a printable booklet of every message left.

## Sending

Press **Send my reply**. The button is disabled until an answer has been chosen.

The confirmation replaces the form:

> **Thank you, Radia** — Wonderful! Your reply has been saved.
>
> You can come back to this link any time before the deadline to change your reply.

A guest who declined sees *"Thank you for letting us know. Your reply has been saved."* instead.

A **Change my reply** button returns to the form.

## If something is rejected

Nothing a guest typed is lost. The reply status, the menu choices, the dietary note and the message are all put back exactly as they were, with the problem shown at the top or against the field concerned. They fix the one thing and send again.

Submissions are rate limited to 30 in fifteen minutes.

## Contributions

If the event asks guests to chip in, two things appear on the invitation page.

**Cost to attend** shows the amount per guest, the deposit and the balance. It is event information, so it is visible before confirming an email address — and on the public page too.

**Your payment** appears only after confirming, because it is personal:

- *"Thank you, your contribution is settled in full."*
- *"Your deposit has been received, with £30.00 to follow."*
- *"Nothing recorded yet. The organiser will let you know how to pay, and will update this once it arrives."*

Guests cannot mark themselves as paid. The organiser records payments as the money arrives; no money moves through Gathered. See [Contributions](/contributions/index).
