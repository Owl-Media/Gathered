# The Guest Experience

This section describes what your guests see. It is written from their side, so you can send them here if they get stuck — or read it yourself to know what to expect.

- [Replying to an Invitation](/guests/replying) — the confirmation step and the reply form.
- [Changing a Reply](/guests/changing-a-reply) — coming back later.
- [After the Deadline](/guests/deadline) — what happens when replies close.

## Opening the link

A guest opens the private link they were sent — from the [invitation email](/invitations/emails), or from a message the organiser sent them directly. The page is designed for a phone first, with large tap targets throughout.

Before confirming anything they can see:

- The banner and profile image.
- **You're invited**, and the event name.
- **When** — the date and times.
- **Where** — the venue and address.
- **Details** — the organiser's description.
- **Cost to attend**, if the event asks for a contribution.
- **On the menu**, as a read-only list of what is being served.

The event details are visible without confirming anything, so a guest can check the date and the address without ceremony.

## Confirming it is them

To reply, they must confirm the email address the invitation was issued to.

![A card reading "Hello Radia — Please confirm the email address this invitation was sent to, so we know it's you", with an email field and a Continue button.](/images/guests/email-gate.png)

The address has to match the one the organiser entered. If it does not:

> The email address does not match this invitation.

The message never reveals the correct address, and never confirms whether the link itself is valid. If a guest is genuinely stuck, the answer is for the organiser to check the address on the [Guests](/events/guests) tab — and if it is wrong, to remove them and add them again with the right one.

Once confirmed, that device stays verified for **two hours**, so the guest is not asked again on every page load.

## No account, ever

Guests do not register, do not choose a password, and cannot sign in. The link plus the email address is the whole mechanism. There is nothing for them to remember and nothing for them to lose but the link itself — and if they lose that, the organiser can send it again.

## What a guest can never see

No page anywhere shows a guest anything about another guest:

- Not other guests' names
- Not who has accepted or declined
- Not how many people are coming
- Not other guests' menu choices, dietary requirements or messages
- Not other guests' links

See [Privacy](/privacy/index) for the complete picture.
