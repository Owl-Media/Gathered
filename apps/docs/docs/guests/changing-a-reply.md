# Changing a Reply

A guest can change their mind as often as they like, right up until the RSVP deadline. The link they were originally sent is all they need.

## Coming back

Opening the same private link again shows the event details, and — after [confirming the email address](/guests/index#confirming-it-is-them) if the two-hour verification window has lapsed — the reply form with everything as they left it:

> **Your current reply:** Accepted. You can change it below until the deadline.

Their previous menu choices are pre-selected, and their dietary note and message are pre-filled. The button reads **Update my reply** rather than *Send my reply*.

## What changing a reply does

An update **replaces** the previous reply. There is no history of earlier answers, and the organiser sees only the current one, along with the time it was last changed.

A few specifics worth knowing:

- **Switching from yes to no clears the dietary note.** A guest who is not coming has no meal, so nothing is carried over. Their message is kept.
- **Switching from no to yes asks for menu choices again**, since none were recorded when they declined.
- **Choices are re-checked against the current menu.** If the organiser has archived an option since the guest first chose it, they are told *"That choice is no longer available for Main. Please choose again."* rather than having something silently picked for them.
- **A new course added since their last reply** must be answered before the update can be saved.

## After an organiser edit

If the organiser has [entered a reply on a guest's behalf](/events/responses#editing-a-reply) — say, after a phone call — the guest still sees that reply when they open their link, and can change it.

Doing so flips the recorded source from **Organiser edited** back to **Guest submitted**, so the organiser can always tell whose answer they are looking at.

The one thing a guest's update never touches is the organiser's [private note](/events/responses#your-private-notes). Guests cannot see it and cannot overwrite it.

## Losing the link

A guest who cannot find their link should ask the organiser, who can press **Send again** on the [Guests](/events/guests) tab. It is the same link, not a new one — an earlier copy in an older email still works.

There is no "email me my link" option on the invitation page. Sending a guest anything is always the organiser's deliberate action.

## After the deadline

Once replies close, changes are no longer possible. See [After the Deadline](/guests/deadline).
