# After the Deadline

Every event has an RSVP deadline — a date and a time, interpreted in the [event's own timezone](/getting-started/create-your-first-event#the-basics), not the guest's and not the server's. "Midnight on the 3rd" means midnight where the event is happening.

## What a guest sees

Opening an invitation link after the deadline still shows the whole event: the images, the date, the times, the venue, the description, the cost and the menu. What has gone is the form.

In its place:

> The RSVP deadline has passed. Please contact the organiser if you need to change your response.

A guest who has already confirmed their email address also sees **Your reply** — their status, their menu choices, their dietary note and their message, all read-only, so they can check what they said.

A guest who has not yet confirmed is still offered the confirmation prompt, so they can look up what they replied.

## What is genuinely not possible

The form is not merely hidden or greyed out — it is not rendered at all, and the action behind it re-checks the deadline on the server on every attempt. A guest cannot submit a late reply by any means.

This applies equally to a first reply and to a change. A guest who never responded stays as **Waiting**.

## What the organiser can still do

Everything.

**Edit any reply directly.** The [Responses](/events/responses#editing-a-reply) tab works exactly the same before and after the deadline. When a guest phones to say they can come after all, the organiser records it, and it is marked as **Organiser edited** so the change is traceable.

**Extend or reopen the deadline.** Change **Replies close on** and **At** on the [Details](/events/details#changing-the-rsvp-deadline) tab. Pushing the date back — including reopening a deadline that passed weeks ago — restores the form for every guest immediately. There is nothing else to switch on.

**Record payments.** [Contributions](/contributions/index) are never gated on the deadline, because money usually arrives after replies have closed.

**Download exports.** All three [exports](/events/exports) work as normal.

## What the organiser sees

The event's card on the [dashboard](/getting-started/dashboard) carries a **Closed** badge, and the event header reads **Replies closed** in red with the date and time.

Nothing is locked, archived or hidden. A closed deadline changes what guests can do, not what the organiser can do.
