# Preview the Guest Journey

Before you send a single invitation, you can see exactly what a guest will see.

From the [Details](/events/details) tab, follow **Preview the RSVP journey**.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** *Preview mode* (`/images/invitations/preview.png`)

## What you see

The preview renders the real page, with the real components, using your event's real details:

- Your header banner and profile image, or your artwork theme.
- **You're invited**, and the event name.
- **When** and **Where**.
- Your description.
- The full reply form, with your menu courses and options.

It starts already past the email confirmation step and with **Yes, I'll be there** selected, so the menu choices are visible without you having to click through. A sample guest name — Sam — stands in for a real one.

A banner across the top makes the mode clear:

> **Preview.** This is what a guest sees after they confirm their email address. Nothing here is saved.

## What it does not do

- **Nothing can be submitted.** The submit button is replaced by a notice, and the action behind it refuses to save regardless.
- **Nobody is emailed.**
- **No guest is created**, and no reply is recorded.

You can open the preview as often as you like, at any stage, and it has no effect on anything.

## What it is good for

- Checking your **menu** reads well as a list of choices, not just as a list in the editor.
- Checking your **description** is legible with its line breaks where you meant them.
- Checking your **images** are cropped the way you expect on a phone — resize your browser window and see.
- Checking the whole thing on a phone before your guests do.

## What it does not cover

The preview starts after the email confirmation step, so it does not show the **Hello *name*** prompt a real guest meets first. See [Replying to an Invitation](/guests/replying) for that.

It also does not show the [contributions](/contributions/index) panels, or the *"Your current reply"* notice a returning guest sees.

If you want the complete journey end to end, add yourself as a guest with your own email address, send yourself the invitation, and walk through it. Remove yourself afterwards and your test reply goes with you.
