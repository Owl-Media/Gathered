# Responses

The Responses tab is your planning view: who has replied, what they chose, what they cannot eat, what they wrote, and your own private notes.

![The organiser responses tab: counts for invited, accepted, declined and not responded, then a filterable list of guests showing each reply, menu choices, dietary requirements highlighted in yellow, messages, and the organiser's private note in blue.](/images/events/responses.png)

## At a glance

Four counts across the top: **Invited**, **Accepted**, **Declined** and **Not responded**. Guests you have removed are excluded from all four.

## Filtering

A row of chips below the counts narrows the list:

| Chip | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Everyone on the guest list |
| **Accepted** | Guests who are coming |
| **Declined** | Guests who are not |
| **Waiting** | Guests who have not replied |
| **Dietary notes** | Anyone who entered an allergy or dietary requirement |
| **Messages** | Anyone who left a message |

Each chip carries its own count, so you can see at a glance how many dietary notes there are to hand the caterer without opening a single card.

## What a reply card shows

- **Name** and **email address**, with a status pill.
- **Menu choices**, one per course — using the names as they were when the guest chose, even if you have renamed or archived them since.
- **Dietary requirements**, in a highlighted yellow block so a nut allergy is not something you can scroll past.
- **Message**, the guest's note to you, in a cream block.
- **Your private note**, in a blue block, visible only to you.
- A footer line: **Guest submitted**, **Organiser edited** or **Not responded**, with the timestamp of the last change.

Guests who have not replied show as a card with no choices and no timestamp.

## Editing a reply

Press **Edit** on any card. This is for the phone call that starts *"I never got round to the link…"*, and for the guest who changes their mind after the deadline.

The edit form gives you:

- **Reply** — Accepted, Declined or Not responded.
- **Menu choices** — one dropdown per course, each defaulting to *Not chosen*. Shown only when the reply is Accepted.
- **Dietary requirements** — shown only when the reply is Accepted, up to 1,000 characters.
- **Guest message** — always available, up to 1,000 characters.
- **Your private note** — up to 1,000 characters, with the reminder: *"Only you can see this. It's left out of exports unless you choose to include it."*

Press **Save changes**.

### The rules are the same as the guest's

Your edits obey exactly the rules a guest's own submission does:

- An **Accepted** guest must have a choice for every course on the menu. Leave one as *Not chosen* and the save is rejected with *"Please choose an option for Main."*
- A **Declined** guest is not asked for menu choices.
- Choices are validated against the menu as it stands right now.

### What gets recorded

Saving marks that reply as **Organiser edited**, along with when, and which organiser did it. That label is shown on the card and included in both the CSV and the planning PDF, so a caterer reading the export can tell a guest's own answer from one taken over the phone.

If the guest later opens their link and updates the reply themselves — before the deadline — it flips back to **Guest submitted**.

### Editing after the deadline

You can edit any reply at any time, including long after replies have closed. There is no need to reopen the deadline for the whole guest list just to correct one person.

## Setting a reply back to Waiting

Choosing **Not responded** clears the reply: the status returns to waiting, and the response timestamp is cleared. Any menu choices are dropped, since a guest who has not replied has not chosen anything.

## Your private notes

The private note is the one field on this tab guests never see, whatever they do. It never appears on their invitation page, in any email, or on the public page. Use it for *"paying in cash on the day"*, *"lift with the Hendersons"*, *"tell the venue about the step"*.

Notes are left out of exports by default. Including them takes a deliberate tick on the [Exports](/events/exports#private-notes) tab, so an export you hand to the venue never leaks them by accident. The keepsake PDF never contains them at all.
