# Menu

The Menu tab defines what guests choose from when they accept. It is entirely optional.

![The Gathered menu builder, with Starter, Main and Pudding courses, each listing options with dietary labels and controls to reorder, edit or remove them.](/images/events/menu.png)

## How the menu works

> Guests who accept must pick one option from every course. If you add no courses at all, guests simply aren't asked about food.

That is the whole rule. A guest who declines is never shown menu choices, and a guest who accepts cannot submit their reply until they have chosen one option for each course.

## Courses

Press **Add a course** and give it a name — *Starter*, *Main*, *Dessert*, or a single course simply called *Food*. Up to 80 characters.

Each course card gives you:

- **↑ and ↓** to move the course up or down. Guests see courses in this order.
- **Rename** to change the course name.
- **Remove** to take it off the menu.

Ordering uses buttons rather than drag and drop, so it works with a keyboard, with a screen reader, and with a thumb on a phone.

::: warning A course with no options is skipped
A course with no options shows a warning: *"This course has no options yet, so guests won't be asked about it. Add at least one."* Guests are not shown it and are not asked to choose from it.
:::

## Options

Within a course, press **Add an option**. Each option has:

- **Option name** — required, up to 120 characters. For example *Roast chicken*.
- **Short description** — optional, up to 240 characters. A line about what comes with it.
- **Dietary label** — optional, up to 40 characters. For example *Vegetarian*, *Vegan* or *Gluten free*. It appears as a small badge beside the option name, on the menu, on the public page and on the guest's form.

Options carry the same **↑ / ↓** reordering, plus **Edit** and **Remove**.

The dietary label is a description of the dish, not a guest's requirement. What a guest can or cannot eat is collected separately, in their own words, on the [reply form](/guests/replying#allergies-and-dietary-requirements).

## Removing and archiving

This is the part worth understanding.

**If nothing has been chosen yet**, removing a course or option deletes it.

**If a guest has already chosen it**, it is *archived* instead. An archived option:

- is no longer offered to guests,
- keeps every reply that already chose it,
- still appears in your [exports](/events/exports), showing what was actually picked.

Archived items are collected at the bottom of the tab — **Archived courses** at the end of the page, **Archived options** within their course — each with a **Restore** button that puts it back in circulation.

This is why a menu can be tidied up mid-event without damaging the replies you already have.

## Renaming after replies have come in

Menu choices are **snapshotted at the moment a guest picks them**. If you rename *Roast chicken* to *Roast free-range chicken* next week, the guests who already chose it still export as having chosen *Roast chicken* — because that is what they saw and agreed to.

The Responses tab and both PDF exports all show the snapshot, not the current name.

## Changing the menu after guests have replied

Replies are re-validated against the menu as it stands **right now**, every time they are saved. In practice:

- Adding a new course after some guests have accepted means those guests have no choice recorded for it. Their existing reply stays valid, but the next time they open their link and update it, they will be asked to choose.
- Archiving a course removes it from the questions asked of future guests, while existing selections are preserved.
- If a guest submits a choice that is no longer available, they are told *"That choice is no longer available for Main. Please choose again."* rather than having something picked for them.

You can fill any gaps yourself from the [Responses](/events/responses#editing-a-reply) tab.

## Where the menu appears

- On the [public event page](/invitations/public-event-page), under **On the menu**, as information — never anyone's selections.
- On a guest's [invitation page](/guests/index) before they verify their email, in the same read-only form.
- On the [reply form](/guests/replying) as the actual choices, once they have verified and said they are coming.
